About SearchBar

A better way to decide where to go.

SearchBar is a hypothetical software company created by SDSU students to make local nightlife information easier to discover, compare, and understand.

Our mission

Make the choice feel simple.

Our mission is to help students make more informed decisions about where to spend their time by bringing useful bar information and student perspectives into one searchable experience.

The problem

Information is scattered.

Students often rely on disconnected social media posts, outdated reviews, and word of mouth. SearchBar organizes the details that matter most before a night out.
