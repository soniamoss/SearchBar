Why we are building a better way to search.

Short reflections from our team about the uniqueness of SearchBar.

Find Your Perfect Night

SearchBar is unique and not a regular review platform because it focuses only on bars and it considers students' experience as it is made by students. We have included information that you usually do not see when looking at reviews or descriptions of a bar. You can really tailor your night out experience through SearchBar and find the bar that fits you and your friend vibe for the night!

Sonia · Team member

Best Bar Match

SearchBar is unique because it’s a bar discovery tool designed around the needs of college students. Instead of making them piece together information from social media, this website would offer one searchable, student-review database that can be filtered by neighborhood, best days to go, music and atmosphere. General review platforms mostly sort by cuisine, price or star rating. SearBar lets users find their best bar match based on the filters.

Amy · Team member

Made for Nightlife in SD

We are building a project titled searchBAR, a web app specifically made to help college kids find bars and nightlife in San Diego. The application will let users search for a bar/club based on the type of music, best days to go, location/neighborhood, ratings and reviews. People will be able to leave reviews about their experience at the bar. Our project is unique because it offers a unique approach to a classic search/discovery problem. We offer unique specific variables to guide search such as music, days to go, etc. I believe our approach of tracking special important values to indicate places to be more favorable is what makes our project unique.

Javier · Team member

For Students, By Students

The project we are building is called “searchBAR” and it is a website/application designed to help college students find fun bars and nightlife spots in the San Diego area. Users have the ability to search for locations and explore them by vibe, music, and other details such as best nights to visit, ratings, and reviews. What makes searchBAR unique is that it is created “for students, by students.” Instead of only providing basic business information, it focuses on the details students care about when deciding where to go with friends. Users can also add new bars and we will possibly be implementing a feature where students can share their experiences, which would allow the site to grow through the student community.

William Kate IV · Team member

More Convenient

SearchBAR is a great way for college students to find bars that match their personal interests and preferences. It not only helps students discover places where they would feel comfortable and enjoy the atmosphere but also gives them the opportunity to meet new people who share similar interests. By bringing people with the same preferences to the same places SearchBAR makes socializing and making new connections easier. It also saves time because students don’t have to spend hours looking for the right bar. Another great feature is that users can stay updated on what’s happening around them, including local events and nightlife activities. Overall, searchBAR makes going out more convenient, social and fun.

Yossan · Team member

Real Students Opinions

What makes SearchBar special is that it focuses on what college students actually care about when choosing a place to go out. Sometimes it is hard to know which bars have good music, a fun atmosphere, or affordable prices. Our website makes this easier by bringing all this information together in one place. I also like that students can share their own reviews and experiences to help others decide where to go. This makes SearchBar more personal and useful because it is based on real student opinions rather than just general ratings.
